### 📈 Business Overview & Strategy

* **Scale & Presence:** OCS is a global leader in **Integrated Facilities Management (IFM)**, employing over 50,000 colleagues in the UK and Ireland with a turnover exceeding **£2bn**.
* **The "BEST" Strategy:** Their current growth is driven by the **"BEST" framework**:
  * **Best Experience:** High-quality service for customers and colleagues.
  * **Best Productivity:** Using technology to drive efficiency.
  * **Best Practices:** Industry-leading safety and compliance.
  * **Best Resilience:** Sustainable and robust operations.
  * **Best Outcomes:** Delivering measurable value to clients.
* **Market Position:** They are currently the **9th largest private employer** in the UK, following a massive merger with Atalian Servest.

---

### 🔄 Recent Mergers & Acquisitions (The "Integration" Challenge)

*As a Solutions Architect, these are your biggest opportunities, as you will likely help integrate these new systems.*

* **EMCOR UK Acquisition (Late 2025):** Added over 7,000 specialist engineers, significantly boosting their **"Hard Services"** (mechanical and electrical engineering) capabilities.
* **Top Security Acquisition (March 2026):** A strategic move in Ireland to enhance **technology-led security services**, including AI-monitored control rooms and remote surveillance.
* **Atalian Servest Merger:** The foundation of the current "Super-OCS," which combined global footprints to create a powerhouse in the UK, Europe, and Asia-Pacific markets.

---

### 💻 Technology & Innovation: "The Digital Business Model"

*OCS is moving away from traditional manual labor and toward a **tech-first approach**.*

* **OCS ABi™ (Artificial Business Intelligence):** Their flagship AI engine that processes data from sensors and smart buildings to provide **predictive maintenance**.
* **Smart Building Integration:** They use IoT sensors to monitor restroom occupancy, air quality, and machinery health (e.g., HVAC systems) to dispatch staff only when needed.
* **Frontline Connectivity:** The **Hapi app** and **OCS Advance** tools ensure that mobile workers stay connected to central systems for task management and safety.

### 🌍 Key Sectors & High-Stakes Environments

*Because they work in these sectors, your **Cyber Security** background is a major selling point.*

* **Healthcare (NHS):** Managing complex hospital environments where system uptime can be a matter of life or death.
* **Aviation:** Providing security and facilities support for major UK airports.
* **Government & Defence:** Handling sensitive sites that require high-level security clearance and robust data protection.
* **Education:** Supporting schools and universities with sustainable, energy-efficient building management.
