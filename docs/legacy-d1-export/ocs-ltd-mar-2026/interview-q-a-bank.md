### 🏢 Category 1: Company Strategy & Vision

*Focus on OCS's growth, values, and the "Digital Business Model."*

✨ **Q: What attracts you to OCS specifically at this stage in our journey?**

* **A:** OCS is at a fascinating tipping point. With a £2bn turnover and the recent scale added by the Atalian Servest merger and EMCOR UK acquisition, the challenge isn't just "maintenance"—it's "integration". I want to help build the **overarching technology architecture** that turns those massive, siloed operations into a **fully automated digital business model**.

✨ **Q: How would you incorporate our TRUE values into your role as an Architect?**

* **A:** In architecture, **Trust** means building resilient, "Secure by Design" systems. **Unity** is critical because this role reports to **Pre-sales** but must collaborate with **Cyber, PMO, and Business Change** to succeed. I see the Architect as the "connective tissue" that brings these teams together.

### ⚙️ Category 2: Role, Governance & TDA

*Focus on how you manage projects, risks, and standards.*

✨ **Q: You'll be leading the architecture and development governance community. How do you handle "Technical Debt"?**

* **A:** I focus on **longevity and solution quality**. During the **Technical Design Authority (TDA)** reviews, I look for "extensible" designs. If a project requires a "quick fix" for a commercial bid, I ensure we document the risk and have a roadmap to align it with our **future state architecture** so it doesn't become a liability later.

✨ **Q: This role reports to the Technical Pre-sales Manager. How does that change your approach to design?**

* **A:** It means the architecture must be a **selling point**. When we bid for a new contract—like a major hospital or airport—our technology needs to show the client that OCS is more efficient and data-driven than the competition. I focus on designing **multi-application solutions** that prove our "Best Productivity" value.

### 💻 Category 3: Technical Depth & Integration

*Focus on the OCS stack: Azure, Bizagi, Maximo, and Salesforce.*

✨ **Q: We use Azure Logic Apps and Bizagi for automation. How does your background fit this?**

* **A:** My experience at Fred. Olsen involves **API-driven integrations** and **event-driven workflows** to reduce manual effort. While I use Cloudflare and Python, the logic of **API mediation** and **serverless compute** (like Azure Functions) is identical. I focus on the **integration pattern**—ensuring data flows securely between systems like **Salesforce and Maximo**.

  \

✨ **Q: What is your approach to complex data onboarding and mapping?**

* \
  **A:** I start by defining the **integration principles**. For OCS, this often involves mapping diverse client data into our core applications. I design **data ingestion pipelines** that are resilient; if a vendor changes their API format, our internal architecture should remain stable.
