# 🧠 Solutions Architect Knowledge Hub

---

## 🏢 OCS Business & Industry Understanding

OCS is a Facilities Management (FM) organisation delivering integrated services across engineering (hard services), cleaning, security, and catering (soft services).

They operate across mission-critical sectors such as Healthcare, Aviation, Government, and Education.

Key idea: They manage entire buildings and operations under a single contract (Integrated Facilities Management – IFM).

👉 Architectural mindset: Design how systems, people, and data connect to support real-world operations.

---

## 🚀 Strategic Direction (Technology Shift)

OCS is moving from a labour-heavy model to a technology-led organisation.

BEST Framework:

* ⭐ Best Experience
* ⚡ Best Productivity
* 🛠️ Best Practices
* 🛡️ Best Resilience
* 🎯 Best Outcomes

👉 What this means for architecture:

* Automation over manual work
* Integrated systems instead of silos
* Data-driven operations

## 🔗 Integrated Business Model (IFM)

Core operational flow:

CRM → Contract → Integration Layer → Operations (Maximo/CAFM) → Workforce → Reporting

Systems involved:

* CRM (Salesforce / Dynamics)
* CAFM (Maximo / Concept Evolution)
* Workforce systems
* Finance systems
* IoT / Sensors

👉 Architect role: Ensure seamless data flow across all systems and eliminate silos.
